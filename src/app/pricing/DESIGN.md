# Pricing page direction

Design read: pricing page for Indonesian developers and agent operators, using Figma pricing's open editorial structure with ScratchAgent's lime accent. ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Color: black surfaces preserve ScratchAgent's identity; readable grey body text and lime controls separate content from actions. FAQ uses a subtle dark green surface.
- Layout: wide editorial hero, two bordered plan columns, comparison table, and split FAQ follow the user's reference while matching ScratchAgent's two real tiers.
- Typography: a neutral sans serif and large, light-weight headings keep prices and limits easy to scan without borrowing Figma's proprietary typeface.
- Spacing: generous hero space creates one focal point; denser plan and comparison sections support factual reading.
- Cards: the plans are flat columns with shared rules, not floating cards, so Free and Pro can be compared without a decorative popularity claim.
- Icons: only the existing ScratchAgent three-bar mark is used; plan content relies on labels and lists.
- Motion: state transitions are limited to hover, billing selection, and FAQ disclosure because this is a decision page, not a product demo.
- Mobile: plan columns stack, controls span the available width, and the comparison table wraps within the viewport.
- Footer: omitted at the user's request; the page ends after the FAQ.
